## What I've Built

### 🏥 AI-Powered Beauty Clinic Ecosystem
> *The most complex project in my portfolio — a full business operating system for a beauty clinic, built from scratch.*

A single platform that runs an entire clinic — not just a booking app, but a complete digital ecosystem:

- **AI Facial Health Detection** — custom-built AI model that analyzes skin condition, identifies concerns and recommends treatments in real time
- **Online Registration & Reservation System** — automated patient intake, scheduling and queue management
- **Marketing Automation** — automated campaigns, follow-ups, and patient retention flows built into the platform
- **Accounting & Finance Module** — revenue tracking, invoicing, expense management — all automated
- **Medical Services Management** — treatment records, doctor workflows, service catalog, patient history
- **One Command, One Ecosystem** — every department operates from a single platform with unified data

> *This project demonstrates what I do at full capacity: complex AI integration, multi-module architecture, automation at scale — all in one production system.*
